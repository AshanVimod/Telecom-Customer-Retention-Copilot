# Retention Offer Policy v1.2 (LankaNet Demo Telecom - fictional)

## 1.1 Purpose and scope
This policy lists every retention offer that staff may make to a customer who is at risk of leaving. Offers not listed in this policy or in the Payment Incentives, Loyalty Programme or Plan Catalogue documents must not be made. All amounts are in the customer's billing currency and apply to the monthly charge.

## 3.1 Contract upgrade discount
Customers on a Month-to-month contract with tenure of 6 months or more may be offered 15% off monthly charges for 12 months if they move to a One year contract. Customers who move to a Two year contract may be offered 20% off monthly charges for 12 months. The discount ends automatically after 12 months.

## 3.2 Free Tech Support add-on
Customers with InternetService = Fiber optic and TechSupport = No may receive the Tech Support add-on free for 6 months. After 6 months the add-on continues at the catalogue price unless the customer cancels it. This offer may be combined with one discount offer.

## 3.3 Limits
Maximum one discount offer per customer per 12 months. Discounts above 20% require supervisor approval and must never be promised to the customer before that approval is recorded. Never promise device replacements, cash refunds or free months of service.

## 3.4 New customer welcome offer
Customers with tenure below 6 months are not eligible for the contract upgrade discount in 3.1. They may instead be offered the Online Security add-on free for 3 months and a welcome call from the onboarding team. No percentage discount applies to these customers.

## 3.5 High bill review
Customers with MonthlyCharges of 80 or more may be offered a free plan review. In the review the agent may move the customer to a lower plan from the Plan Catalogue without any change fee. A plan review is not a discount and does not count toward the limit in 3.3.

## 3.6 Senior citizen support
Customers with SeniorCitizen = 1 may be offered priority phone support at no charge and a simplified paper bill on request. This may be combined with any other offer in this policy.

## 3.7 When no offer applies
Customers on a One year or Two year contract are already receiving contract pricing and are not eligible for 3.1. If the churn risk level is low, no retention offer should be made. If no offer in this policy applies, tell the customer about the Loyalty Programme and record the contact.
