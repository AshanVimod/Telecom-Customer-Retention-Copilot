# Retention Team FAQ v1.0 (LankaNet Demo Telecom - fictional)

## 7.1 Can two discounts be combined?
No. Only one discount offer per customer per 12 months is allowed. A discount may be combined with non-discount benefits such as the free Tech Support add-on, the automatic payment credit, a plan review or senior citizen support.

## 7.2 What if the customer asks for a bigger discount?
The largest discount staff can offer without approval is 20%. A request above 20% must be escalated to a supervisor and must not be promised.

## 7.3 Which languages can messages be sent in?
Messages may be sent in English, Sinhala or Tamil, according to the customer's preference. Translated messages must be reviewed by a staff member who reads that language before sending.

## 7.4 Does the system send messages automatically?
No. Every recommendation and message is a draft. A retention officer must review and approve it before anything is sent to a customer.
