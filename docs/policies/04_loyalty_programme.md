# Loyalty Programme v1.1 (LankaNet Demo Telecom - fictional)

## 5.1 Membership
Every customer with tenure of 12 months or more is a member automatically. There is no fee and no sign-up.

## 5.2 Tenure rewards
Members with tenure of 24 months or more receive one free add-on of their choice for 3 months each year. Members with tenure of 48 months or more receive a free speed upgrade to the next plan level for 3 months each year. Tenure rewards do not count toward the discount limit in the Retention Offer Policy.

## 5.3 Referral reward
A member who refers a new customer receives a one-time bill credit of 10 after the new customer's first paid invoice.
