# Payment Method Incentives v1.0 (LankaNet Demo Telecom - fictional)

## 2.1 Automatic payment credit
Customers with PaymentMethod = Electronic check or Mailed check who switch to Bank transfer (automatic) or Credit card (automatic) receive a one-time bill credit of 5 on the next invoice. The credit is applied once per customer and is not a discount under the Retention Offer Policy limits.

## 2.2 Paperless billing
Customers with PaperlessBilling = No who switch to paperless billing receive 1 off the monthly charge for as long as paperless billing stays active. This may be combined with the automatic payment credit in 2.1.

## 2.3 Failed payment handling
If an automatic payment fails, the customer is contacted by SMS and given 7 days to pay before any late fee. Staff must not waive late fees as a retention offer; late fee waivers follow the Complaint Handling Guide.
