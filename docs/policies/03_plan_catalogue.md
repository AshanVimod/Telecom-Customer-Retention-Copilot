# Plan Catalogue v2.0 (LankaNet Demo Telecom - fictional)

## 4.1 Internet plans
DSL Basic costs 25 per month. DSL Plus costs 35 per month. Fibre Standard costs 70 per month. Fibre Max costs 95 per month. Customers may move down one plan level without a change fee during a plan review.

## 4.2 Add-ons
Each add-on costs 5 per month: Online Security, Online Backup, Device Protection and Tech Support. Streaming TV and Streaming Movies cost 10 per month each. Add-ons can be removed at any time with no fee.

## 4.3 Phone service
Phone service costs 20 per month for a single line. Multiple lines cost 5 per additional line per month.

## 4.4 Contract terms
Month-to-month contracts can be cancelled at any time. One year and Two year contracts carry an early termination fee equal to one month's charge. Contract pricing for One year and Two year terms is already 5% and 10% below the Month-to-month price.
