# Complaint Handling Guide v1.0 (LankaNet Demo Telecom - fictional)

## 6.1 First response
Acknowledge every complaint within 1 working day. Apologise for the inconvenience, restate the problem in the customer's words and give a reference number.

## 6.2 Service outage credit
If a customer had no service for more than 24 continuous hours, credit one day of the monthly charge for each full day without service. Outage credits are not retention offers.

## 6.3 Late fee waiver
A late fee may be waived once per 12 months for a customer with no other late payment in that period. Record the waiver on the account.

## 6.4 Escalation
Escalate to a supervisor when the customer asks to cancel, when a discount above 20% is requested, or when the same complaint is raised a third time.

## 6.5 Tone for written messages
Messages must be polite, short and free of pressure. Never state or imply that the customer is likely to leave. Never mention a churn score, risk level or prediction to the customer.
